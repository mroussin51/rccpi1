Single vs multi‑account setup

Use one account for very small teams; move to multi‑account for production isolation, billing clarity, and stronger guardrails. Start with a management account and separate prod from non‑prod when you hit multiple apps or clients.

Steps

  1. Start in one account with orgs enabled.
  2. Split prod/non‑prod as complexity grows.
  3. Apply SCPs and guardrails centrally.
  4. Standardize tagging across accounts.

Onboard/offboard safely

Use SSO with short‑lived roles. Grant access by job function, not person. For departures, revoke SSO access, rotate shared secrets, and verify no lingering keys exist. Keep a checklist and an owner for each step.

Steps

  1. Provision via SSO; assign roles by function.
  2. Use groups; avoid individual policies.
  3. Automate offboarding; rotate credentials.
  4. Audit quarterly for dormant access.

A tagging strategy that pays off

Tag everything for owner, environment, app, and cost center. Enforce tags in CI and report spend by tag. This makes cost reviews, cleanup, and compliance dramatically simpler.

Steps

  1. Define required tags: Owner, Env, App, CostCenter.
  2. Validate tags in CI and at deploy time.
  3. Generate monthly cost by tag reports.
  4. Clean untagged resources on a schedule.
