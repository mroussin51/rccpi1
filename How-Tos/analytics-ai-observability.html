Analytics, AI & Observability for Small Teams

On this page

Minimal analytics without tracking drama

Use a privacy‑respecting analytics tool for page views and conversions, and tie key events to business outcomes. Avoid over‑collecting data; leaders want trends and actions, not noise. Keep a simple weekly dashboard everyone can read.

Steps

  1. Define the 3–5 metrics that matter.
  2. Instrument events tied to outcomes.
  3. Publish a weekly dashboard for stakeholders.
  4. Review trends; pick one action per week.

Use AI to summarize logs and alerts

Route logs to a central store and summarize errors daily. Use anomaly detection to surface unusual spikes. Keep human‑readable runbooks so on‑call can act quickly when AI flags an incident.

Steps

  1. Centralize logs and metrics.
  2. Enable anomaly detection and summary jobs.
  3. Link alerts to runbooks with owners.
  4. Review false positives and tune rules monthly.

Dashboards leadership actually reads

Show uptime, latency, top errors, conversion rate, and monthly cloud spend on one page. Add a short narrative and one decision request. Update weekly and archive to track progress over time.

Steps

  1. Pick 5 tiles max: A, L, E, CR, Cost.
  2. Write a 3–4 sentence summary.
  3. Propose one decision or tradeoff.
  4. Archive snapshots to show trends.
