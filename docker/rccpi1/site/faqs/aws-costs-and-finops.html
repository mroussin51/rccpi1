How can I control and reduce AWS costs without hurting performance?

Right‑size instances, turn off idle environments, use savings plans for steady loads, apply S3 lifecycle policies, and tag everything. Review budgets and anomalies monthly and act on waste. Continuously measure cost per unit (visit, order, job) to keep spend tied to business value.
