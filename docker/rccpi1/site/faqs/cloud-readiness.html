What is cloud readiness and how do I assess it?

Cloud readiness evaluates your apps, data, security, and team to decide if, when, and how to move to the cloud. A quick assessment documents your current state, clarifies goals, identifies risks, and produces a phased roadmap—pilot, migrate, then optimize—aligned to uptime, cost, and recovery targets.

Cloud Readiness & Strategy

A fast, practical readiness check tailored for small businesses.

Readiness checklist

  • Inventory apps, data, identities, and integrations
  • Classify data sensitivity and retention requirements
  • Define success metrics (uptime, latency, costs, recovery)
  • Choose landing zone: single AWS account vs. multi‑account
  • Decide “lift‑and‑shift” vs. “refactor” per workload
  • Plan zero‑downtime cutovers and a rollback path

Fast assessment workshop (2–4 hours)

  • Current state: apps, pain points, constraints
  • Target state: security, reliability, and budget
  • Gap analysis: quick wins and blockers
  • Roadmap with phases: pilot → migrate → optimize
How long does migration usually take?

A basic brochure website can move in hours; a multi‑app environment may take days to weeks depending on data, integrations, and testing.

What skills do we need in‑house?
