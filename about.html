Who we are

RCCPI (Rooster Cloud Computing Pi) is a hands‑on consultancy focused on pragmatic cloud architecture, migrations, and day‑2 operations. We’re builders who explain things in plain English and leave you with working systems—not black boxes.

How we work

  1. Listen: goals, constraints, timeline.
  2. Plan: right‑sized architecture with clear costs.
  3. Build: Docker/Kubernetes or KVM, IaC when it helps.
  4. Harden: TLS, MFA, least‑privilege, backups, monitoring.
  5. Support: documentation and training so you stay in control.
  • Start small with a pilot (static site/CDN or backup tier).
  • Rollback plan for every cutover.
  • Monthly health report: uptime, errors, cost.

What we do

Cloud consulting & migrations • Containers & VMs • Reverse proxy & SSL • Security & compliance • Cost optimization • Training

Values

  • Clarity over jargon.
  • Reliability through redundancy and tested restores.
  • Security by default—MFA, encryption, least privilege.
  • Performance because fast sites convert.
  • Local first: based in Raleigh, NC.

Credentials

Hands‑on with Ubuntu, Docker, Kubernetes, KVM, and the major clouds. Studying/holding CompTIA tracks (Linux+, Network+, Security+). We document everything we deploy so your team can run it day‑to‑day.
